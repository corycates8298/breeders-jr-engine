# Breeders & Jr Engine

**Live site:** https://corycates8298.github.io/breeders-jr-engine/

This is the handicapping engine for the 2026 Breeders' Cup at Keeneland (Oct 30–31). It combines:
- the market (morning line or live odds);
- expert consensus;
- Jr's method;
- Claude's model, built on 40 required factors.

It also tracks results and grades every pick source.

## What's on the site
- **Dashboard:** every race with its post time (in ET and in your time zone), Claude's pick, the market favorite, the experts' pick, Jr's pick and the result.
- **Race pages:**
  - overview and situation and pace;
  - horse-by-horse detail and the 40-factor matrix;
  - Jr vs consensus;
  - exactas and trifectas;
  - **Bet check:** expected value at live odds, the minimum odds worth betting, and quarter-Kelly stakes with caps. PASS is the usual answer.
- **Results & review:** a scoreboard of locked pre-race picks, the market, the experts and Jr, with the model's refit history.

## This copy is read-only
- **Data:** every result through the Classic, as of Nov 1, 2026.
- **Your changes stay in your browser only:** what-ifs, the Bet check readiness list and your bankroll aren't saved for anyone else.
- **Race-day updates** (scratches, live odds, results) are made on the live engine. This copy is refreshed from it.
- **Not indexed:** the site asks search engines not to index it.

## Disclaimer
This is decision support, not betting or financial advice. Every win chance is an estimate, and even a correctly priced 40% horse loses 6 times in 10.
